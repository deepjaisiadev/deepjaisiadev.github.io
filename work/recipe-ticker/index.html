Recipe Ticker

People Inc. (Formerly Dotdash Meredith)

A continuously updating recipe ticker for MyRecipes.com, built while learning Vue.js on a hard deadline.

  • 2M+ DAU
  • 50 items in memory, max
  • Infinite-feeling scroll with two 25-item lists: fill the off-screen list while the other is in view, then snap seamlessly.
  • Only 50 items in memory at any time, however long someone scrolls.

Stack: Vue.js, Virtualised scrolling
