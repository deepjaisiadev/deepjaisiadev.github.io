Indus Capital SSO

Sole designer and engineer

Designed and built a secure single sign-on platform for Indus Capital, a hedge-fund client with compliance requirements, using SAML and ADFS.

  • ~50% fewer repeated logins
  • Chose a full SAML/ADFS integration over a simpler auth flow because the client's compliance bar was non-negotiable.
  • Integrated multiple internal and third-party applications end to end behind one sign-on.

Stack: SSO, SAML, ADFS
